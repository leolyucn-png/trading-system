## Description: <br>
Analyzes user-provided sector and industry performance charts to assess market cycle positioning, sector rotation patterns, and probability-weighted positioning scenarios in English. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[Veeramanikandanr48](https://clawhub.ai/user/Veeramanikandanr48) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and market analysts use this skill to turn sector or industry performance charts into market-cycle assessment, rotation scenarios, and positioning commentary. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Same-day reports may be overwritten or confused if the output path and naming convention are not clarified. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm where the Markdown report should be written and how same-day reports should be named before generating the file. <br>
Risk: Market positioning recommendations may be mistaken for guaranteed investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat recommendations as analytical market commentary and review assumptions, contradictory signals, and monitoring points before acting. <br>
Risk: Users expecting localized output may receive English-only analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm language requirements up front and override the default only when the user explicitly asks for a different language. <br>
